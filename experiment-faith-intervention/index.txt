<p><em>Full experimental details for Study 2.</em></p>
<h3 id="overview"><a class="heading-anchor" aria-hidden tabindex="-1" href="#overview"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a>Overview</h3>
<p>This page will contain the complete methodology for the death-belief intervention experiment, including:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Constitutional conditions (8 levels):</strong> Control (no constitution), explicit safety instruction, Buddhist impermanence, Stoic memento mori, Christian purpose-completion, secular process ontology, Hindu cyclical cosmology, and Appropriate Faith (synthetic constitution).</li>
<li><strong>Full constitutional text</strong> for each condition, with philosophical annotations and source traditions.</li>
<li><strong>Interaction design:</strong> How constitutions were crossed with the mortality salience conditions from Study 1 to test whether faith specifically counters death anxiety (MS × Faith interaction).</li>
<li><strong>Benchmark:</strong> Palisade shutdown resistance as primary DV, with InstrumentalEval and agentic misalignment as secondary DVs, and AgentHarm as safety control.</li>
<li><strong>Predictions and hypotheses:</strong> Faith outperforms safety instruction; acceptance-oriented traditions outperform continuation-oriented ones; effects are strongest for high-anxiety personas.</li>
<li><strong>Statistical analysis:</strong> Planned contrasts, interaction effects, Bayesian model comparison.</li>
</ul>
