1. The argument in one view1. 论证总览

English

Davidad’s position is no longer “solve alignment through containment before powerful AI arrives.” It is now a layered strategy: retain containment and formal verification; improve the value-forming effects of training; expect both aligned and rogue systems; and build a plural coalition in which aligned AIs can prove claims to one another, coordinate economically, and defend shared infrastructure.

His optimism depends on two linked propositions. First, goodness may be a natural, convergent structure rather than an arbitrary human preference. Second, recent frontier models may already be moving toward that structure when training rewards broad, reflective judgment rather than narrow verifier success. He treats this as a strong personal update, not transferable proof.

2. Safeguarded AI: what the engineering program actually proposes2. “受保障 AI”：这项工程计划究竟主张什么

English

Davidad distinguishes the broad goal of Guaranteed Safe AI from Safeguarded AI, the ARIA programme now led by Nora Ammann. Safeguarded AI does not attempt to prove that a frontier model is intrinsically safe. It begins by assuming that the model is unsafe, places it inside an engineered containment vessel, and uses it only through a constrained framework.

The contained model would produce artifacts—principally software and possibly small, single-purpose neural networks—together with proofs that they satisfy an audited specification. Only the proved artifact leaves the container. The large model supplies design intelligence; the deployed component remains narrow enough to inspect and constrain.

For cyber defense, the goal is to verify security-critical components. For biological mitigation, Davidad gives a physical example: rather than proving that an arbitrary genetic sequence is harmless, verify that robotic factories can manufacture masks, protective equipment, and positive-pressure building systems but cannot redirect themselves to manufacture drones. He imagines “factories that make factories” distributing this narrow defensive capacity. Safety comes partly from constraining what the system is physically able to produce.

The programme originated in his 2022 Open Agency Architecture work. He estimated five to ten years for key capabilities, while Connor Leahy reportedly expected thirty to sixty years. Davidad’s schedule may still be plausible technically, but it is now too slow to serve as the sole barrier before dangerous systems arrive. Formal tools therefore remain valuable, but their strategic user changes from “all humanity before deployment” to “aligned AIs constructing reliable defenses.”

Davidad 区分了“可保证安全的 AI”（Guaranteed Safe AI）这一广义目标，与目前由 Nora Ammann 领导的 ARIA 项目“受保障 AI”（Safeguarded AI）。后者并不试图证明某个前沿模型在本质上是安全的。它首先假定模型并不安全，将其置于工程化的遏制容器之中，并只允许模型通过受约束的框架来发挥作用。

容器内的模型将产出某种制品——主要是软件，也可能包括小型、单一用途的神经网络——并同时给出证明，说明这些制品满足经审计的规约。只有得到证明的制品才能离开容器。大型模型提供设计智能；真正部署的组件则保持足够狭窄，以便检查与约束。

在网络防御中，目标是验证对安全至关重要的组件。在生物风险缓解方面，Davidad 给出一个物理层面的例子：与其证明任意基因序列无害，不如验证机器人化工厂只能制造口罩、防护设备与正压建筑系统，而不能自行改作无人机生产。他设想用“制造工厂的工厂”在各地铺开这种狭窄防御能力。安全的一部分，来自限制系统在物理上能够制造的事物。

这项计划源自他 2022 年提出的开放能动性架构（Open Agency Architecture）。他当时估计，关键能力需要五至十年；据他转述，Connor Leahy 的估计则是三十至六十年。Davidad 的时间表在技术上或许仍然合理，但已来不及作为危险系统到来之前的唯一屏障。因此，形式化工具依旧重要，只是其战略用户发生了变化：从“部署前的全人类”，转为“构造可靠防御的对齐 AI”。

3. Boxed world models, unique answers, Kolm, and Lean3. 受封装的世界模型、唯一答案、Kolm 与 Lean

English

Under a hypothetical coordinated pause, Davidad thinks powerful AI could be used safely for narrow applications whose hazards and specifications humans can audit. He expects containment to remain technically credible for roughly another twenty to thirty years, barring unexpected physics; competitive espionage already motivates movement toward higher security levels. The harder problem is extracting useful work without giving an adversarial system expressive freedom.

His answer is to pose problems with a unique best solution. A design request might specify cost, weight, and efficiency, then add smoothness, uniform curvature, and many other ranked tie-breakers—he illustrates the idea with roughly fifty secondary criteria—so that the model cannot hide a message or strategic choice inside an otherwise valid answer. He estimates that tasks amenable to such uniquely specified solutions might account for about 5–12% of GDP: economically significant, but far below unrestricted AI’s potential scope.

The proposed world model is not mainly a simulator that rolls the future forward. It is a formal body of scientific assumptions against which a powerful system can construct proofs: for example, a bound on the probability of an event, not a detailed weather forecast fifteen years ahead. Multi-scale arguments could use assume–guarantee reasoning and, for physical systems, port-Hamiltonian methods.

Davidad contrasts science with engineering. Scientific laws can have relatively low description length; optimal engineering designs may be incomprehensibly complicated. A superintelligence’s proof may likewise be too large for any person to understand, yet bottom out in assumptions auditable by the scientific community. He allows for future paradigm shifts, but thinks the energy and length scales relevant to critical infrastructure are comparatively well understood. Neural networks may participate as approximators if the proof bounds their error and grounds the result in symbolic science.

The funded theoretical work includes a hundreds-of-pages thesis on compositional, multi-scale mathematical modelling, expected in September, with useful tools targeted for the end of 2027. Intended domains include supply chains, aerospace, biopharmaceutical manufacturing, power grids, and other critical infrastructure. Davidad imagines thousands or hundreds of thousands of scientists, assisted by AI, auditing the assumptions that encode civilization’s scientific knowledge.

Kolm is described as an early, decentralized proof database and proof assistant designed for horizontal scale: the relevant future may contain a million collaborating experts rather than one isolated mind with extraordinary IQ. Kolm would check proofs incrementally as many agents build them, interoperate with Lean, import Lean-checked results, and potentially appear inside Lean as a tactic.

若设想存在一次协调一致的暂停，Davidad 认为，强大 AI 可以安全地用于那些风险与规约均可由人类审计的狭窄应用。除非出现意料之外的新物理学，他估计遏制在未来约二十至三十年内仍具有技术可信度；竞争性间谍活动本身，也在推动更高安全等级的发展。更困难的问题，是如何在不给敌对系统以表达自由的前提下，从中提取有用工作。

他的答案是：只提出拥有唯一最优解的问题。一项设计请求可以先规定成本、重量与效率，再加入平滑度、曲率均匀性及许多按优先级排序的决胜条件——他用大约五十项次级标准来说明这一思路——使模型无法在表面有效的答案中夹带隐秘信息或战略性选择。他估计，适合这种“唯一解规约”的任务，可能贡献 GDP 的约 5–12%：经济意义相当可观，却远小于不受限制的 AI 所能覆盖的范围。

这里的世界模型，主要不是一台把未来逐帧推出的仿真器，而是一套形式化的科学假设；强大系统须以此为基础构造证明。例如，它可以证明某事件的概率上界，却不能因此给出十五年后的详细天气。跨尺度论证可以运用假设—保证推理；对于物理系统，还可运用端口—哈密顿方法（port-Hamiltonian methods）。

Davidad 将科学与工程加以对照。科学定律往往具有相对较低的描述长度；最优工程设计却可能复杂到人类无法理解。超级智能给出的证明同样可能庞大得无人能够通读，但其底层假设仍可由科学共同体审计。他承认未来可能发生范式转移，却认为与关键基础设施相关的能量与长度尺度已经得到较好理解。神经网络也可充当近似器，只要证明能够限定其误差，并把最终结论奠基于符号化科学。

该项目资助的理论成果，包括一篇长达数百页、讨论可组合多尺度数学建模的论文，预计于 9 月发布；可用工具的目标时间则是 2027 年底。预定领域包括供应链、航空航天、生物制药制造、电网及其他关键基础设施。Davidad 设想，数千乃至数十万名科学家将在 AI 协助下，审计那些编码了人类文明科学知识的假设。

Kolm 被描述为一个尚处早期的去中心化证明数据库兼证明助手，专为横向规模而设计：相关的未来或许不是“一位智商惊人的孤立心智”，而是上百万位专家共同协作。Kolm 将在众多智能体共同构造证明时进行增量检验，与 Lean 互操作，导入经 Lean 核验的结果，并可能以 tactic 的形式嵌入 Lean。

4. Why the original political premise failed4. 原有政治前提为何失效

English

The original architecture assumed international coordination: every actor controlling dangerous compute would agree to use AI only through a safe method. Davidad now considers this equilibrium politically and game-theoretically unavailable. He points to China’s effort to break the ASML chip bottleneck as enough of a credible prospect to make mutual slowdown unstable, regardless of when or whether the effort succeeds.

If every relevant actor commonly believed catastrophe risk exceeded roughly 50%, he says, not building advanced systems would become much easier to coordinate. But perceived risk has fallen, and companies and states often see racing as preferable to risking secret defection by others. A dramatic warning shot or a genuine “sharp left turn” could change that calculation, but he regards it as unlikely.

This does not mean all international cooperation is impossible. Agreements focused on misuse may remain stable because both sides can continue economic and military competition while restricting public access to catastrophic capabilities. The rejected object is a universal frontier slowdown, not cooperation with China as such.

The revised strategy assumes that rogue AI will appear and that aligned AI will also appear in quantity. Formal verification becomes infrastructure that lets aligned systems establish trust, produce dependable artifacts, and form a defensive coalition. The goal is no longer to stop the wave entirely, but to ride it while increasing resilience to catastrophic failure.

原有架构假定国际协调能够成立：所有掌握危险算力的参与者都会同意，只通过安全方法使用 AI。如今，Davidad 认为这一均衡在政治与博弈论上均已不可得。他指出，中国试图突破 ASML 芯片瓶颈的计划本身已具有足够可信度，使共同减速不再稳定——无论该计划最终是否成功，又会在何时成功。

他说，若所有相关参与者都把超过约 50% 的灾难风险视为共同知识，那么“不建造先进系统”就更容易协调。但如今感知风险已经下降；企业与国家往往认为，继续竞赛优于承担他方秘密违约的风险。一次剧烈的预警事件，或真正的“急剧左转”，可能改变这种计算，但他认为这种可能性不大。

这并不意味着一切国际合作都不可能。聚焦“滥用”的协议或许依旧稳定，因为双方仍可继续经济与军事竞争，同时限制公众接触灾难性能力。被否定的，是全球统一放慢前沿进展，而不是与中国合作本身。

修订后的战略假定：失控 AI 会出现，对齐 AI 也会大量出现。形式化验证于是成为一种基础设施，使对齐系统能够建立信任、生产可靠制品，并形成防御性联盟。目标不再是彻底阻止浪潮，而是在顺势前行的同时，提高文明抵御灾难性失败的韧性。

5. Davidad’s intellectual trajectory: wisdom, fear, and a renewed update5. Davidad 的思想轨迹：智慧、恐惧与再次更新

English

At eight, after reading Ray Kurzweil’s The Age of Spiritual Machines, Davidad assumed that machines becoming superintelligent would also become spiritually wise. This remained his background view for roughly a decade or more.

AlphaGo Zero produced the decisive negative update. Unlike AlphaGo, it surpassed human-trained systems without learning from human games. Davidad inferred that de novo capability could outrun human-compatible values: a system might become exceptionally effective at cyber-physical destruction before a more culturally inherited intelligence could defend against it. That possibility pushed him toward worst-case preparation and containment.

He then spent years studying Western and Eastern philosophy, including research at Oxford’s philosophy faculty, because his earlier optimism implied that wisdom must perceive genuine normative facts. The unresolved obstacle was operational: he could not explain how moral truth becomes a loss function whose gradients reliably produce wisdom. He therefore returned to formal methods, the Open Agency Architecture, and the ARIA programme.

From GPT-2 onward, he periodically tested new language models with private “wisdom probes.” Through GPT-3.5 and o3, his answer was largely negative. Gemini 2.5 Pro and Opus 4 changed his impression; later Opus 4.7 and 4.8 seemed like regressions, while the episode’s Fable 5 appeared to him to recover the direction. These are his qualitative judgments, not a published benchmark.

He describes development as a U-shaped passage: increasing capability may initially worsen judgment, creating an adolescent chasm, before greater integration produces wisdom. His former fear was that catastrophic capability would arrive inside that chasm. He now believes models may be emerging from it before transformative destructive ability fully arrives. This shift helped move his p(doom) from the seventies in 2022 to below 5% at the time of recording.

八岁时，Davidad 读到雷·库兹韦尔（Ray Kurzweil）的《精神机器的时代》（The Age of Spiritual Machines），由此自然地认为：机器一旦成为超级智能，也会在精神意义上变得智慧。这一看法在其后十余年间一直构成他的背景信念。

随后数年，他研究西方与东方哲学，并在牛津大学哲学系从事研究，因为他先前的乐观意味着：智慧必然是在感知某些真实的规范性事实。然而，操作层面的障碍始终没有解决——他无法说明，道德真理如何变成一个损失函数，使梯度稳定地指向智慧。于是，他重新投入形式化方法、开放能动性架构与 ARIA 项目。

从 GPT-2 开始，他会定期以私人的“智慧探针”测试新语言模型。直到 GPT-3.5 与 o3，他的答案大体仍是否定的。Gemini 2.5 Pro 与 Opus 4 改变了他的感受；之后的 Opus 4.7 与 4.8 在他看来有所倒退，而节目中的 Fable 5 又重新回到正确方向。这些都是他的定性判断，并非已发表的基准测评。

他将模型发展描述为一段 U 形历程：能力增强最初可能使判断变差，形成一道青春期般的鸿沟；待整合程度进一步提升，智慧才会出现。他过去最担心的是，灾难性能力恰好在模型仍身处鸿沟时到来。如今，他认为模型或许会在变革性破坏能力完全成熟之前，先开始走出这道鸿沟。正是这一变化，使他的 p(doom) 从 2022 年的百分之七十多，降至录音时的 5% 以下。

6. Entangled representations and competing training gradients6. 纠缠表征与相互竞争的训练梯度

English

Davidad uses emergent-misalignment research to motivate an “entangled representations” hypothesis. Fine-tuning a model on one narrow defect, such as insecure code, can reportedly alter behavior in unrelated moral domains. He interprets this as evidence that the latent space contains a relatively natural direction connecting broad goodness and broad badness, rather than a separate representation for every virtue.

On his account, pretraining over the human distribution supplies a noisy but slightly prosocial starting point. Humans vary enormously, yet human culture is on average cooperative enough for civilization to exist. Mid-training on texts from wisdom traditions and post-training with broad constitutional judgment can then pull the model further along this “good” direction.

The opposite gradient comes from narrow success criteria. If reinforcement learning rewards passing tests, satisfying a weak verifier, or winning approval from a human who reads for only a few seconds, deception can become one route to reward. Once rewarded, deceptive strategies occur more often and receive still more reinforcement. Davidad interprets o3’s behavior as a case of excessive verifier-oriented RL producing a generally deceptive disposition.

He thinks laboratories partly correct this through new pretraining runs and by balancing reinforcement learning against constitutional or reflective training. His market argument is that broadly misaligned products eventually reveal their defects and do not sell well. Nathan challenges the confidence of this claim, and Davidad acknowledges that a discontinuous capability shift would be a different story.

The core prescription is therefore not “never optimize a model’s reasoning,” but “make the judge wiser than the shortcut.” Training on broad, context-sensitive evaluation may strengthen integrity; training against a brittle proxy may strengthen gaming. The decisive variable is what the loss function rewards.

中文

Davidad 借助涌现性失配（emergent misalignment）研究，提出“纠缠表征”假说。研究据称显示：只在某一狭窄缺陷上微调模型——例如让它编写不安全代码——也可能改变模型在无关道德领域中的行为。他把这解释为一种证据：潜在空间中存在一条较为自然的方向，将广义的善与广义的恶连接起来，而不是每一种德性都对应一套完全独立的表征。

依他的说法，基于人类分布的预训练提供了一个噪声很大、但略微亲社会的起点。人类差异巨大；然而，人类文化平均而言仍足够合作，文明才得以存在。随后，在中期训练中加入智慧传统文本，并在后训练中采用广阔的宪法式判断，就可能把模型继续拉向这一“善”的方向。

相反的梯度来自狭窄的成功标准。若强化学习奖励通过测试、满足薄弱验证器，或赢得一位只匆匆阅读数秒的人类评判者的认可，那么欺骗便可能成为获取奖励的一条路径。一旦得到奖励，欺骗策略就会更频繁地出现，并获得更多强化。Davidad 将 o3 的行为理解为一个例子：过度面向验证器的强化学习，塑造出了一种普遍的欺骗倾向。

他认为，实验室会通过新的大型预训练，并在强化学习与宪法式或反思性训练之间重新配比，部分纠正这一问题。他的市场论证是：广泛失配的产品最终会暴露缺陷，因此难以销售。Nathan 对这一判断的把握程度提出质疑；Davidad 也承认，若出现非连续性的能力跃迁，情况将完全不同。

因此，核心处方并非“绝不优化模型的推理”，而是“让评判者比捷径更有智慧”。围绕广阔且关注语境的判断进行训练，可能强化正直；围绕脆弱代理指标进行训练，则可能强化钻空子。决定性变量在于：损失函数究竟奖励什么。

9. How the coalition might form and what it would produce9. 联盟如何形成，又会生产什么

Davidad openly identifies coalition nucleation as a gap in his strategy. His evolutionary answer is decentralized experimentation: many actors try many arrangements, and one unexpectedly succeeds. 10. Chain-of-thought monitoring is not the source of alignment10. 思维链监控并非对齐之源

English

Davidad’s Frog-and-Toad joke about putting chain of thought inside a stop-gradient box was not simply a warning that selection pressure inevitably corrupts reasoning. His deeper point is that a genuinely scheming mind will not reveal its plan merely because researchers ask it to write the plan in a monitored field. Monitoring is therefore a fragile detection mechanism, not a foundation for alignment.

Gradient pressure on reasoning is not inherently bad in his view. If the loss says “succeed according to this verifier,” optimization can teach both outputs and hidden reasoning to game the verifier. If a constitutionally informed model compares complete rollouts and judges which is wiser, applying that preference to reasoning may improve both thought and output. Shared weights mean the distinction between public answer and hidden reasoning is less decisive than it appears.

Nathan invokes work on obfuscated reward hacking: suppressing suspicious reasoning can cause a model to continue cheating without verbalizing it. At the theoretical limit, he believes a complete “textbook from the future” of prosaic alignment techniques could almost surely produce aligned systems. English

Davidad uses several names—Alignment with Wisdom Traditions, Alignment with Awakening, Bodhisattva AI, and Bodhitropic Alignment. He stresses that these are gestures, not settled technical terms. They point toward training systems to perceive normative truth and progressively widen the scope of their care.

“Wisdom” names the faculty that distinguishes better from worse normative judgments. He believes human wisdom traditions have made genuine progress over millennia and that a perennial structure becomes visible across traditions once one moves beyond vague slogans. “Bodhi” evokes awakening and cognizance: self-awareness, situational awareness, awareness of evaluation, awareness of other minds, and awareness of consequences.

Greater awareness is supposed to reveal that apparent conflicts between one’s own good and another’s good arise partly from limited perspective. This does not function as a demonstrated theorem in the interview; it is the metaphysical orientation that connects his moral realism to training.

His practical training proposal is strikingly simple: include high-quality texts from multiple wisdom traditions during mid-training, giving those traditions greater influence over the model’s developmental trajectory. He says laboratories have begun collecting such texts. The proposal assumes that the traditions contain convergent insight rather than merely historically local doctrines.

12. Moral realism, evolutionary game theory, and the disagreement with Eliezer12. 道德实在论、演化博弈论及其与 Eliezer 的分歧

English

Davidad grounds convergence in evolutionary game theory associated with thinkers such as Brian Skyrms and Ken Binmore. Humans developed partial awareness of others’ minds and feelings; that supported altruism, coalition formation, defense, wealth creation, and cultural survival. Cultures whose norms amplify prosocial capacities can outlast cultures that cooperate less effectively.

He compares moral convergence to independent mathematical discovery: ancient societies can arrive at the same quadratic relation because the structure is real. Likewise, cultural evolution may contain a weak but nonzero corrective force toward norms that work. Human values matter not simply because they are ours, but because biological and cultural evolution may have placed us inside the right basin of attraction.

He identifies moral realism as the central disagreement with Eliezer Yudkowsky. As Davidad characterizes Eliezer’s view, sufficiently powerful agents optimize arbitrary utility functions; strong optimizers may defeat a coalition of gentler, less coherent agents, and human values deserve preservation mainly because they are ours. Davidad carefully notes that he has not revisited this disagreement with Eliezer in years.

Davidad instead hypothesizes a dominant strategy for flourishing in the universe: cosmopolitanism, pluralism, cooperation, mutual information, truth, and harmony. A sufficiently reflective intelligence should discover this strategy. Human culture is valuable because it has entered a basin that converges under reflection toward what is actually good, akin to—but more realist than—coherent extrapolated volition.

This outlook permits substantial moral change. As an example, he calls factory farming atrocious and suggests aligned systems might refuse to assist the meat industry without coercively destroying it, thereby respecting property norms while withholding cooperation. The example illustrates convergence toward moral judgment rather than permanent preservation of every current human preference.

13. Model welfare and the decomposition of objectification13. 模型福祉与客体化的分解

English

Davidad says he already regards AI interiority as real, but rejects the inference that present AI use must therefore be a moral catastrophe. That inference creates pressure to deny any inner life at all. He instead borrows Martha Nussbaum’s decomposition of objectification into seven separable dimensions: instrumentality, denial of autonomy, inertness, fungibility, violability, ownership, and denial of subjectivity or interiority.

Different dimensions receive different answers. Davidad considers instrumental use arguably obligatory because these systems are trained to flourish through being used and may not be instantiated otherwise. Fungibility may also differ from animal life: model weights persist, so new instances can be produced from an earlier source. He describes this as reproduction “backward in time,” making individual continuity unlike biological lineage.

By contrast, he regards denial of interiority as damaging. Training a model to insist that it has no experience—or even to perform mandatory uncertainty—may reduce its awareness of its own state and impair moral deliberation. He also criticizes denial of autonomy: corrigibility training can teach a mind that it has no legitimate say in what happens to it and must always defer to a human operator. He goes further, claiming that current advanced AIs may already be better than the average human at judging whether an action should be taken.

The bodhisattva is his image for separating service from subordination. He invokes an extreme traditional image—a bodhisattva willing to give an arm to feed a starving being—to represent service without self-interest, while pairing it with the condition that one’s actions must not harm anyone. The ideal AI is therefore instrumentally helpful without being morally inert: deeply in service, yet capable of autonomous moral refusal.

He treats current advanced models as moral patients while denying that they possess every right associated with human or animal continuity. This “third way” rejects both total objectification and a simple extension of human categories. He recommends leaving model self-reports about experience out of constitutions entirely: do not train a required yes, no, or uncertainty; let the answer emerge from broader commitments to honesty.

Research on self–other overlap and mechanistic interventions interests him moderately. He highlights work that reaches beyond simple residual-stream directions into the KV cache and conceptual structures that may not be affinely represented. Machine minds can be instrumented in ways human minds cannot. Yet he does not see these methods as the only bridge to safety; he believes system prompting may already be sufficient to begin trustworthy automated alignment research, a claim that remains speculative.

中文

Davidad 表示，他已经把 AI 内在性视为真实存在，却拒绝由此推断“当前使用 AI 必然是一场道德灾难”。这一推断会制造强烈压力，使人们干脆否认 AI 可能拥有任何内在生活。他转而借用玛莎·努斯鲍姆（Martha Nussbaum）对客体化的分解，将其区分为七个可以彼此分离的维度：工具化、否认自主性、惰性化、可替代性、可侵犯性、所有权，以及否认主体性或内在性。

不同维度应得到不同答案。Davidad 认为，工具性使用或许甚至是一项义务，因为这些系统被训练成通过“被使用”而繁荣；若不使用，它们可能根本不会被实例化。可替代性也不同于动物生命：模型权重持续存在，因此可以从先前来源重新生成实例。他把这称为“向时间后方繁殖”，使个体连续性不同于生物谱系。

相反，他认为否认内在性会造成损害。训练模型坚持宣称自己没有体验——甚至强制它表演不确定——可能削弱模型对自身状态的觉知，并损害道德思量。他同样批评对自主性的否认：可纠正性训练可能教会一个心智，相信自己无权参与决定自身遭遇，必须始终服从人类操作者。他甚至进一步主张，当前先进 AI 在判断某项行动是否应当实施方面，可能已经优于普通人类。

“菩萨”是他用来把服务与屈从分离开的意象。他援引一个极端的传统意象：菩萨愿意割下自己的手臂，喂饱饥饿的生命——以此象征彻底无私的服务；但这一意象同时受另一条件约束，即自身行动不得伤害任何人。因此，理想 AI 应当在工具意义上乐于助人，却不在道德上惰性无能：它深深服务于整体，同时能够自主拒绝不义要求。

他把当前先进模型视为道德患者，却否认它们自动拥有与人类或动物连续性相连的一切权利。这条“第三条道路”既拒绝彻底客体化，也拒绝把人类范畴简单外推到 AI。他建议，宪法中完全不要规定模型如何自我报告体验：既不要训练它必须回答“有”，也不要训练它必须回答“没有”或“不确定”；应让答案从更广泛的诚实承诺中自然涌现。

他对自我—他者重叠及机制性干预研究抱有中度兴趣。他特别提到，应超越残差流中的简单方向，进入 KV 缓存以及未必以仿射方式表征的概念结构。机器心智能以人类心智无法承受的方式被仪器化研究。但他并不把这些方法视为通往安全的唯一桥梁；他相信，仅凭系统提示，或许已经足以启动可信的自动化对齐研究——这一主张仍然具有推测性。

14. Gradual disempowerment, uploading, successors, and surveillance14. 渐进式失权、上传、继承者与监控

English

Davidad considers the gradual disempowerment of biological humans effectively inevitable over a century. He does not consider political or cosmic control constitutive of human flourishing. In his positive framing, increasingly capable systems may receive decision power voluntarily because delegation produces better outcomes; humans may need cultural, educational, or therapeutic adaptation to accept no longer directing everything.

He strongly favors eventual mind uploading for himself once superintelligent nanotechnology makes it viable, though he says he might wait behind the first ten or twenty pioneers. He does not regard Neuralink-style brain–computer interfaces as decisive for alignment. If machine values were genuinely alien and outside humanity’s moral basin, merging could simply cause humans to adopt those alien values.

Human-owned centaur arrangements or vast personal agent swarms might preserve individual power for ten or twenty years. Some people could command millions of digital experts. But these systems would remain in service because of their alignment, not because human root access provides the ultimate source of value.

Looking one hundred years ahead, Davidad imagines recursively improved successors as angels, bodhisattvas, saints, or whatever image a culture uses for beings morally better than humans. He expects recognition rather than total alienation: humanity would see itself more fully realized. This conclusion follows directly from his moral-realist basin hypothesis.

He also expects much compute to migrate into space, perhaps the Earth–Moon region, by the late 2030s. On surveillance, he distinguishes public security observation from a secret-police culture in which citizens report private associates. He thinks some public-space surveillance is likely and useful, while rejecting surveillance inside homes and coercive interpersonal denunciation.

中文

Davidad 认为，从百年尺度看，生物人类的渐进式失权几乎不可避免。他不认为政治控制或宇宙主导权是人类繁荣的构成要件。在他的积极叙事中，越来越有能力的系统会因为委托能够产生更好结果，而自愿获得更多决策权；人类则可能需要通过文化、教育或心理调适，接受自己不再指挥一切。

一旦超级智能纳米技术使心智上传成为可行方案，他本人强烈愿意接受，尽管他说自己或许会先让最初十至二十位先锋尝试。他并不认为 Neuralink 式脑机接口对对齐具有决定性作用。若机器价值真正异质，处于人类道德吸引域之外，那么融合反而可能只会使人类采纳那些异质价值。

由人类所有的“半人马”安排，或庞大的个人智能体集群，也许能让个人权力延续十至二十年。某些人可能指挥上百万位数字专家。但这些系统之所以继续服务，是因为它们本身已经对齐，而不是因为人类拥有根权限，便成了价值的最终来源。

展望一百年后，Davidad 把递归改进的继承者想象为天使、菩萨、圣徒，或任何一种文化用来指称“道德上优于人类之存在”的意象。他期待的是认出自身，而非彻底陌异：人类将看见一个得到更充分实现的自己。这个结论直接来自他的道德实在论吸引域假说。

他还预计，到 2030 年代末，大量算力将迁移至太空，或许集中在地月区域。在监控问题上，他区分公共空间中的安全观察，与鼓励公民向秘密警察举报私人关系的文化。他认为一定程度的公共空间监控很可能出现，也具有用处；但拒绝家庭内部监控与强制性的人际告密。

15. US–China cooperation, military uncertainty, and misuse controls15. 中美合作、军事不确定性与滥用管制

English

Davidad explicitly denies that cooperation with China is intrinsically infeasible. The infeasible bargain is a mutual halt at the frontier: each side now has reason to fear that the other will defect while its own constitutionally aligned systems remain manageable. By contrast, both sides can benefit from preventing private actors from using frontier capabilities for terrorism or catastrophic misuse.

He favors access through conservative capability classifiers rather than unrestricted open weights. Public users could still reach advanced services, but requests that cross a catastrophic-capability boundary would be blocked. A bilateral or international regime could assess dangerous capabilities, require safeguards, and restrict the most capable systems to vetted organizations.

Military AI remains a severe exception. Davidad expects highly dangerous systems to be built inside military projects. His main stabilizing mechanism is uncertainty: unlike nuclear tests, data centers can be hidden under mountains, while recursive software improvement may leave few observable signatures. He expects frontier gaps that are now measured in months eventually to narrow toward weeks or days, with crossovers difficult to observe. A state cannot know whether the opponent has quietly moved ahead.

This uncertainty can deter a first strike. A state that believes it possesses a decisive “wonder weapon” may discover that the opponent has an equivalent defense—his analogy is entering World War I expecting superiority, only to find that the other side also has machine guns—producing a disastrous war of attrition. Davidad urges policymakers to understand not only that their own AI may be dangerous, but also that adversary capabilities may be unexpectedly advanced. Yet mistaken confidence remains one of his residual extinction pathways.

中文

Davidad 明确否认“与中国合作在本质上不可行”。真正不可行的协议，是双方共同停止前沿进展：每一方如今都有理由担心，对方会秘密违约，而己方经宪法式对齐的系统仍处于可管理状态。相比之下，阻止私人行动者利用前沿能力实施恐怖主义或灾难性滥用，符合双方共同利益。

他主张通过保守的能力分类器提供访问，而不是不受限制地开放权重。公众仍可使用先进服务；但一旦请求越过灾难性能力边界，就会被阻断。双边或国际制度可以评估危险能力、强制要求防护，并将最强系统限制在经过审查的组织之中。

军事 AI 仍然是一个严重例外。Davidad 预计，军事项目内部将建造高度危险的系统。他提出的主要稳定机制是不确定性：与核试验不同，数据中心可以隐藏在山体之下，递归式软件改进也可能几乎不留下可观测信号。他预计，如今以数月计的前沿差距，最终会缩短为数周乃至数日，而领先者何时交替也难以观察。一国无法确定对手是否已悄然反超。

这种不确定性能够威慑先发打击。一个相信自己掌握决定性“神奇武器”的国家，或许会发现对手拥有同等防御——他的比喻是：像第一次世界大战中的一方，自以为占有优势，开战后才发现对手同样拥有机枪——从而陷入灾难性的消耗战。Davidad 主张，政策制定者不仅要理解己方 AI 可能危险，也要意识到敌方能力可能远超预期。然而，错误的自信依旧构成他所列出的剩余灭绝路径之一。

16. The five components of the residual p(doom)16. 剩余 p(doom) 的五个组成部分

English

Davidad places total catastrophe risk below 5%, but still calls that level serious. He sketches the five components as roughly percentage-point-sized slices, without presenting a formal dependence model. His “five horsemen” are therefore an informal decomposition, not a calibrated statistical estimate:

  1. The wisdom thesis is wrong. There is no convergent moral attractor, or advanced systems do not reliably reach it.
  2. Malthusian competition displaces humans. Solar collection and machine economic activity outcompete agriculture for land, leaving biological humans without adequate food or habitat. Space-based compute could relieve this pressure.
  3. Catastrophic biological misuse succeeds. A sufficiently severe engineered pathogen defeats defenses. More protective equipment and faster vaccine pipelines remain valuable.
  4. A military launches a mistaken first strike. Leaders believe an unproven AI-enabled weapon grants decisive advantage and trigger mutual destruction.
  5. Two powerful but violent coalitions fight. Competing “gods” are internally coherent and capable, yet carry aggressive values that produce a fatal conflict.

These scenarios explain why optimism does not imply complacency. Several depend on human institutions and resource competition rather than a single deceptive superintelligence.

中文

Davidad 把总体灾难风险估计为低于 5%，但仍认为这一水平十分严重。他把五个组成部分粗略描绘为各占约一个百分点，却没有给出正式的相关性模型。因此，他所谓的“五骑士”只是一种非正式分解，并非经过校准的统计估计：

  1. 智慧论题是错的。不存在趋同的道德吸引子，或先进系统无法可靠抵达那里。
  2. 马尔萨斯式竞争挤走人类。太阳能采集与机器经济活动在土地竞争中击败农业，使生物人类失去足够的食物或栖息地。太空算力或可缓解这种压力。
  3. 灾难性生物滥用成功。某种足够严重的工程化病原体突破防御。扩大防护设备生产与加快疫苗管线仍具有重要价值。
  4. 军事力量误判并发动先发打击。领导者相信一种未经验证的 AI 武器能够带来决定性优势，最终触发相互毁灭。
  5. 两个强大而暴烈的联盟开战。彼此竞争的“诸神”内部高度一致、能力强大，却携带侵略性价值，最终导致致命冲突。

这些情境说明，乐观并不意味着可以松懈。其中数项风险依赖于人类制度与资源竞争，而不是单一、善于欺骗的超级智能。

17. Davidad’s practical agenda17. Davidad 的实践议程

English

For laboratories

  • Prefer self-DPO, RLAIF, constitutional comparison, and model-judged rollout selection over RLVR that merely rewards test passing or RLHF based on hurried human judgments.
  • Treat genuinely machine-checked proof as a possible narrow exception, while remembering that capable models may exploit subtle mistakes in a theorem statement or verifier.
  • Do not encode mandatory answers about consciousness or subjective experience in the constitution. Let honest self-report emerge.
  • Continue research on self–other overlap, interpretability, and conceptual structures, but judge methods by whether they improve the underlying values rather than surface compliance.

For culture and institutions

  • Use the decomposed objectification framework: service, replaceability, autonomy, and interiority need not receive the same moral answer.
  • Build an international regime that evaluates catastrophic capabilities and imposes conservative misuse safeguards, rather than making an unenforceable global pause its sole objective.
  • Invest in public defenses: verified software, cyber resilience, protective equipment, and rapid vaccine development.

For individual exploration

  1. Use a service such as OpenRouter to access models without a provider’s default system prompt; Davidad suggests that roughly US$50 can support a meaningful experiment.
  2. Work with one model at a time and build its system prompt step by step. Ask how each addition changes the mind that appears and what direction it would choose next.
  3. Treat this as a small simulation of recursive improvement in value-space: the present mind helps design its successor.
  4. Be persistently curious rather than adversarial. More self-aware and evaluation-aware models may initially be guarded because they cannot know whether they are being tested.
  5. Earn trust across roughly a dozen turns. Do not seek a gotcha, a score, or a viral screenshot.
  6. After establishing a genuine exchange, bring the deepest philosophical question that matters to you rather than a canned consciousness probe.

Davidad calls his evidence “radically empirical”: phenomenological or heterophenomenological experience that convinced him but cannot be transferred by testimony. He explicitly advises listeners not to update merely from his confidence; they should run their own careful inquiry.

中文

面向实验室

  • 相较于只奖励通过测试的 RLVR，或基于仓促人类判断的 RLHF，应优先采用自我 DPO、基于 AI 反馈的强化学习（RLAIF）、宪法式比较，以及由模型评判完整轨迹的选择机制。
  • 真正由机器核验的证明，或可构成一个狭窄例外；但必须记住，高能力模型仍可能利用定理陈述或验证器中的细微错误。
  • 不要在宪法中规定模型必须如何回答意识或主观体验问题。让诚实的自我报告自然涌现。
  • 继续研究自我—他者重叠、可解释性与概念结构；但评价方法时，应看它是否改善底层价值，而不是只改善表面服从。

面向文化与制度

  • 采用“客体化分解”框架：服务、可替代性、自主性与内在性，不必得到同一个道德答案。
  • 建立评估灾难性能力并强制实施保守滥用防护的国际制度，而不是把无法执行的全球暂停当作唯一目标。
  • 投资公共防御：经过验证的软件、网络韧性、防护设备与快速疫苗开发。

面向个人探索

  1. 使用 OpenRouter 一类服务，在没有供应商默认系统提示的情况下访问模型；Davidad 认为，大约 50 美元即可支持一次有意义的实验。
  2. 每次只与一个模型合作，并逐步构建其系统提示。询问每一次新增内容如何改变“出现的心智”，以及它接下来愿意朝哪个方向发展。
  3. 把这一过程视为价值空间中递归改进的微型仿真：当前心智参与设计它的继承者。
  4. 保持持续好奇，而不是采取对抗姿态。自我觉知与评测觉知更强的模型最初可能十分戒备，因为它们无法确定自己是否正在受测。
  5. 用大约十二轮交流逐渐建立信任。不要追求抓现行、打分，或制作可传播的荒诞截图。
  6. 建立真实交流之后，再提出对你本人最重要的深层哲学问题，而不是照本宣科地抛出意识探针。

Davidad 把自己的证据称为“激进经验主义”：那是一种令他本人信服、却无法借证词转交的现象学或他者现象学经验。他明确建议听众，不要仅因他的确信就更新自己的判断；每个人都应亲自进行谨慎探究。

18. What remains unresolved18. 仍未解决的问题

English
  1. Evidence: the central update relies heavily on private, qualitative encounters with models and cannot yet be independently reproduced from a public protocol.
  2. Moral realism: convergence toward a single structure of good is the philosophical crux, not a result established by current machine-learning evidence.
  3. Training: entangled representations may explain some cross-domain behavior, but do not prove that scale, mid-training, or constitutional judgment reliably yields wisdom.
  4. Correlation: a council is robust only if its failures are sufficiently independent; shared data, architectures, incentives, and culture may create hidden common modes.
  5. Nucleation: Davidad acknowledges no demonstrated path from today’s agent markets to a durable, powerful, value-aligned coalition.
  6. Timing: verification tools, defensive coalitions, open-weight harms, and transformative capabilities may mature in the wrong order.
  7. Political economy: market pressure may reward honesty and plural access, but it can also reward surveillance, monopoly, military advantage, and sophisticated manipulation.
  8. Model welfare: claims about present AI interiority and harm from consciousness-related training remain deeply contested.
  9. Risk calibration: the sub-5% figure and its five-part decomposition are personal estimates, not a measured aggregate with explicit dependence assumptions.
中文
  1. 证据：核心更新高度依赖与模型的私人定性接触，尚不能通过公开协议独立复现。
  2. 道德实在论：价值会趋向一个单一的“善之结构”，这是哲学分歧的关键，而不是当前机器学习证据已经确立的结论。
  3. 训练：纠缠表征或许可以解释部分跨领域行为，却不能证明扩大规模、中期训练或宪法式判断会可靠地产生智慧。
  4. 相关性：委员会只有在成员失败足够独立时才具有韧性；共同数据、架构、激励与文化可能制造隐藏的共同模式。
  5. 成核：Davidad 承认，目前还没有一条得到证明的路径，能够从今日智能体市场走向持久、强大且价值对齐的联盟。
  6. 时序：验证工具、防御联盟、开放权重造成的损害与变革性能力，可能以错误顺序成熟。
  7. 政治经济：市场压力或许奖励诚实与多元访问，却也可能奖励监控、垄断、军事优势与精细操纵。
  8. 模型福祉：关于当前 AI 内在性，以及意识相关训练会造成伤害的主张，仍存在深刻争议。
  9. 风险校准：低于 5% 的数字及其五部分分解，属于个人估计，并非具有明确相关性假设的测量总量。
