The Machine Consciousness Hypothesis机器意识假说

English
中文

What is consciousness, and how does it relate to reality?—is arguably among the most interesting open questions in science and philosophy. Making progress on it is of importance to culture, because it will help us to understand our nature, past, present and potential. It is relevant to our ethics, for instance when we relate to non-human agents, both biological and artificial, which may or may not be sentient. It has technological applications, like the creation of interfaces that can meaningfully extend human agency and cognitive capacity, can help to reduce suffering in medical and psychotherapeutic contexts, and may advance our self-awareness, skills and psychological development. Gaining an understanding of consciousness may also be relevant to future minds—potentially sentient artificial intelligences, which will find themselves to coexist with human civilization and life on earth—and to determine prudent, practical, and ethical boundaries around the creation of such entities.

意识是什么，它与实在又有何关系？这可以说是科学与哲学中最引人入胜的开放问题之一。推动这一问题取得进展，对文化至关重要，因为它将帮助我们理解自身的本性、过去、现在与潜能。它也与伦理息息相关，例如，当我们面对非人类智能体——无论是生物的还是人工的，而它们可能有感知，也可能没有——我们该如何与之相处。它还具有技术应用：可以促成真正延展人类能动性与认知能力的界面；可以帮助减轻医疗和心理治疗情境中的痛苦；也可能增进我们的自我觉知、技能与心理发展。理解意识，还可能关系到未来的心智——可能具有感知能力的人工智能，它们将发现自己须与人类文明及地球生命共存——并有助于我们为创造这类存在划定审慎、切实且合乎伦理的边界。

Why is such an important question met with so little organized effort to resolve it? Understanding consciousness seems to pose unique difficulties for the tools and epistemology of modern science. Many philosophers speak of the ‘Hard Problem’ when they refer to the challenge of reducing conscious experience to physical processes, and even use terms like ‘ineffable’ (impossible to describe), ‘intrinsic’ (immediately given and not analyzable), or ‘irreducible’ (not reproducible by a coarse grained causal model of substrate behavior) to describe its futility. The apparent difficulty to characterize consciousness in scientific terms has led to a retrenchment in psychology, which is today less concerned with the psyche than with observable and measurable behavior, to stagnation and pessimism in philosophy of mind, and to reluctance in large parts of neuroscience to even address the topic.

如此重要的问题，为何几乎没有得到有组织的解决努力？对于现代科学的工具与认识论而言，理解意识似乎构成了独特的困难。许多哲学家在谈到将意识经验还原为物理过程的挑战时，会称之为“难题”（Hard Problem）；甚至用“不可言说”（无法描述）、“内在”（直接给定且不可分析）或“不可还原”（无法由关于基底行为的粗粒化因果模型再现）等词，来形容这种努力的徒劳。意识似乎难以用科学术语刻画，这使心理学退守——如今它所关心的，与其说是心灵，不如说是可观察、可测量的行为——也令心灵哲学陷入停滞与悲观，并使神经科学的许多领域甚至不愿触及这一主题。

Making sense of the ‘Hard Problem’理解“难题”

中文

The ‘Hard Problem’ describes the difficulty of explaining the phenomenology of consciousness by reducing it to mechanistic interactions within a physical, biological, or abstract computational substrate. Though coined more recently as a term by David Chalmers1, the issue has been described by many others, such as Thomas Nagel in his famous essay ‘What Is It Like to Be a Bat?’2, or by Joseph Levine in 1983, who calls it the “explanatory gap”3. But already three centuries ago, in his Monadology, Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz gives an illustration4:

“难题”指的是这样一种困难：若把意识还原为物理、生物或抽象计算基底内部的机械相互作用，便难以解释意识的现象学。这个术语虽是戴维·查默斯（David Chalmers）较近才提出的1，但许多人都描述过同一问题，例如托马斯·内格尔（Thomas Nagel）在其名篇《成为一只蝙蝠会是什么样？》中所述2；约瑟夫·莱文（Joseph Levine）则在 1983 年称其为“解释鸿沟”3。然而早在三个世纪前，戈特弗里德·威廉·莱布尼茨（Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz）便已在《单子论》中作出如下说明4：

“[W]e must confess that perception, and what depends upon it, is inexplicable in terms of mechanical reasons, that is through shapes, size, and motions. If we imagine a machine whose structure makes it think, sense, and have perceptions, we could conceive it enlarged, keeping the same proportions, so that we could enter into it, as one enters a mill. Assuming that, when inspecting its interior, we will find only parts that push one another, and we will never find anything to explain a perception.”

“我们必须承认，知觉以及依赖于知觉的一切，都不能由机械原因来解释，也就是说，不能凭形状、大小和运动来解释。假如我们设想有一台机器，它的结构使之能够思考、感觉并产生知觉；那么我们可以设想，在保持比例不变的情况下将它放大，大到我们可以像走进磨坊一样走入其中。即便如此，当我们检视它的内部时，所见也只会是彼此推动的部件，而绝不会发现任何足以解释知觉的东西。”

Centuries later, John Searle’s Chinese Room illustrates a similar gap in his perspective on computational systems5, replacing Leibniz’ machine with a computer that mechanically responds to sequences of Chinese symbols with appropriate responses. By picturing himself as an agent mechanically following the instructions of the algorithm, and noting his own lack of understanding Chinese, Searle highlights the apparent absurdity of attributing mental qualities (like understanding) to a computer, an intuition that persists even in the face of today’s large language models (LLMs), which demonstrate the practical feasibility of Searle’s provocative thought experiment.

数百年后，约翰·塞尔（John Searle）的“中文房间”以他对计算系统的看法，说明了类似的鸿沟5：莱布尼茨的机器被一台计算机取代，后者机械地读取一串串中文符号，并给出恰当回应。塞尔把自己设想为一个机械执行算法指令的智能体，同时指出自己并不理解中文，由此凸显出把“理解”等心智属性归于计算机似乎何其荒谬。即使面对今天的大语言模型（LLM），这种直觉依然存在——而大语言模型恰恰展示了塞尔这一挑衅性思想实验在实践上的可行性。

Curiously, the ‘explanatory gap’ seems to be a problem specific to the modern scientific (Western) world view. For most of human history and in many places outside of science, the dominant world view can be characterized as animism, the belief that living nature is animated by spirits that are intrinsically agentic and capable of experience. Aristotelianism—the starting point of much of Western science, psychology and rationality—may also be understood as animist: Aristotle describes the soul, spirit, or psyche, as a form (causal structure)6 of the physical substrate that is its animating property.7 8

耐人寻味的是，“解释鸿沟”似乎是现代科学（西方）世界观所特有的问题。在人类历史的大部分时期，以及科学之外的许多地方，占主导地位的世界观都可以称为泛灵论：相信鲜活的自然由灵体赋予生机，而这些灵体本身具有能动性与经验能力。作为西方科学、心理学和理性主义诸多传统起点的亚里士多德主义，也可以被理解为泛灵论：亚里士多德把灵魂、精神或心灵描述为物理基底的一种形式（因果结构）6，这种形式正是使基底获得生机的属性。7 8

He distinguishes a vegetative soul, common to plants and the bodies and animals, which is concerned with growth and nurture, the perceiving soul of animals, capable of decision making and controlling behavior, and the reasoning intellect that is unique to humans.

他区分出三类灵魂：植物以及动物身体所共有、关乎生长与滋养的营养灵魂；动物所具有、能够作出决定并控制行为的感觉灵魂；以及人类独有的理性理智。

Western culture, however, came to disavow animism, first under the influence of the mechanist philosophers of the early Enlightenment, such as Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz, and LaMettrie, which sought to free scientific rationality from the influence of religious doctrine and mythological conceptions of the soul, and more decisively under the influence of scientific positivism in the 20th century, which struck entities that could not be measured or observed from the scientific dictionary, and metaphysical discussions from the curriculum. In psychology, the influence of positivism led to a dominance of behaviorist paradigms; in philosophy, to a divorce between analytic thought (which tended to focus on problems and arguments suited to formal approaches) and continental/hermeneutic traditions (which addressed experience, but lost their footing in natural science and rationalism).

然而，西方文化后来开始摒弃泛灵论。最初的影响来自启蒙运动早期的机械论哲学家，如笛卡尔、斯宾诺莎、莱布尼茨和拉·梅特里；他们试图使科学理性摆脱宗教教义及神话式灵魂观念的影响。到了 20 世纪，科学实证主义的作用更具决定性：一切无法测量或观察的实体都被从科学词典中删去，形而上学讨论也被逐出课程。在心理学中，实证主义的影响使行为主义范式占据主导；在哲学中，它促成了分析思想与欧陆／诠释学传统的分裂——前者往往聚焦适合形式化方法的问题与论证，后者虽探讨经验，却失去了在自然科学和理性主义中的立足点。

The ‘Hard Problem’ and the ‘explanatory gap’ may be symptoms of our metaphysical malnourishment. Contemporary discussions of ‘free will’, the ontological status of perception, the nature of the elements of experience (which philosophers call qualia) and of consciousness often fail to notice that we are regularly confronted not just with one, but at least three notions of reality. Feelings, such as will and the experience of freedom or realness itself, are part of our psychological reality—they are representations within the mind. The functional mechanisms that produce the psychological reality—our mind, personality, motivational dynamics and so on—are part of a causal reality, which also encompasses things like money and software. Physics, when understood as the interactions of matter and energy, constitutes a third reality, or more precisely, a special case of a causal model. What makes it special is the hypothesis of physicalism: physics defines a causally closed, mechanistic lowest level of nature, and physicalism claims that everything we observe and interact with is coarse grained patterns we identify in the dynamics of this mechanical, physical reality. While foundational physics is considered incomplete, and progress on its completion has arguably stalled after the formulation of the Standard Model in the 1970s, the predictions of physical theories are so extraordinarily accurate that physicalism has become the dominant paradigm of the scientific worldview. Yet consciousness, minds, and mental states lie outside of the domain of physics.

“难题”与“解释鸿沟”或许是我们形而上学营养不良的症状。今天关于“自由意志”、知觉的本体论地位、经验要素（哲学家称之为感受质）以及意识本性的讨论，往往没有注意到：我们经常面对的并非一种实在观念，而至少有三种。意志、自由感或真实感本身等感受，属于我们的心理实在——它们是心智内部的表征。产生这种心理实在的功能机制——我们的心智、人格、动机动力学等等——则属于因果实在；金钱与软件之类的事物也包含在其中。若把物理学理解为物质与能量的相互作用，它便构成第三种实在；更准确地说，是因果模型的一个特例。其特殊性来自物理主义假说：物理学定义了一个因果闭合、机械论的自然最低层级；物理主义则主张，我们观察并与之互动的一切，都是从这一机械的物理实在之动力学中辨识出的粗粒化模式。尽管基础物理学被认为尚不完备，而且自 20 世纪 70 年代标准模型形成以来，补全它的进展可以说已陷入停滞，但物理理论的预测如此惊人地精确，以至于物理主义已成为科学世界观的主导范式。然而，意识、心智与心智状态都处在物理学的领域之外。

The constituents of physical entities—particles, matter, energy, wavefunctions—are mathematical models of regularities in information (discernible differences) about the universe. In a sense, physicists are studying how the universe processes information. The idea that information, not matter and energy, should be seen as fundamental, has led John Archibald Wheeler to coin the slogan “it from bit”.9

物理实体的构成要素——粒子、物质、能量、波函数——都是关于宇宙的信息（可辨识的差异）之规律性的数学模型。从某种意义上说，物理学家研究的是宇宙如何处理信息。认为应当把信息而非物质与能量视为根本的观念，促使约翰·阿奇博尔德·惠勒（John Archibald Wheeler）创造了“万物源于比特”（it from bit）这一口号。9

Meanwhile, computer scientists have begun to rediscover the idea of spirits. As Harold Abelson puts it: “Computational processes are abstract beings that inhabit computers. (...) In effect, we conjure the spirits of the computer with our spells. A computational process is indeed much like a sorcerer’s idea of a spirit. It cannot be seen or touched. It is not composed of matter at all. However, it is very real. It can perform intellectual work. It can answer questions. It can affect the world by disbursing money at a bank or by controlling a robot arm in a factory. The programs we use to conjure processes are like a sorcerer’s spells.”10 While Abelson is speaking metaphorically here, many of the founders of the fields of Artificial Intelligence and Cognitive Science argue that the human mind can be literally understood as a computer program11, enacted by the communication patterns between biological cells.

与此同时，计算机科学家开始重新发现“灵体”这一观念。正如哈罗德·阿贝尔森（Harold Abelson）所说：“计算过程是栖居于计算机中的抽象存在。……实际上，我们用咒语召唤计算机的灵体。计算过程确实很像巫师观念中的灵体：它不可见，也不可触；它根本不由物质构成。然而，它又极为真实。它可以从事智力工作，可以回答问题；它可以通过银行付款或控制工厂里的机械臂来影响世界。我们用来召唤进程的程序，就像巫师的咒语。”10 阿贝尔森在此固然是在打比方，但人工智能与认知科学领域的许多奠基者都主张，人类心智可以从字面上被理解为一种计算机程序11，由生物细胞之间的通信模式付诸实现。

It is crucial to understand that a computer program is not defined by its language or specific hardware, but as an abstract causal pattern, a dynamic mathematical structure that can evolve through complex sequences of states when imprinted on a suitable physical substrate, and thereby influence the course of the physical universe. Computer programs are also not simply a way to talk about the configurations of distinctly observable physical objects, such as electrons in transistors, any more than money is a way to talk about ink molecules bonded to the cellulose of bank notes. They are a meaningful invariance, a coarse pattern that persists over many possible perturbations and configurations of the substrate (such as the arrangements of molecules of the transistors, the circuit layouts of logical gates, the design and architectures and specifications of computer hardware, and oftentimes underlying layers of different programs). This invariant pattern has causal power, because its implementation (the way in which it is imprinted on the substrate) leads to control of the substrate. Reducing the pattern to the specific substrate dynamics that implement it will obscure this invariance of causal control, and deprive us of understanding how reality is going to evolve at the level we care about.

至关重要的是，我们要理解：计算机程序并不由其语言或某种特定硬件来定义，而是一种抽象的因果模式——一种动态数学结构；当它被铭刻到适当的物理基底上时，可以历经复杂的状态序列而演化，并由此影响物理宇宙的进程。计算机程序也不只是谈论显著可观察物理对象之配置的一种说法——正如金钱并不是谈论钞票纤维素上附着的油墨分子的一种说法。程序是一种有意义的不变量，一种粗粒化模式；它在基底可能经历的诸多扰动与配置中持续存在（例如晶体管的分子排列、逻辑门的电路布局、计算机硬件的设计、架构与规格，以及常常位于其下的不同程序层）。这种不变模式具有因果力量，因为它的实现方式（即它如何被铭刻到基底上）会带来对基底的控制。若把这一模式还原为实现它的具体基底动力学，便会遮蔽这种因果控制的不变性，也使我们无法理解：在我们关心的层级上，实在将如何演变。

Of course, computer programs written by human engineers to run on digital hardware look very different from the evolving, self-sustaining, self-reproducing, error-correcting codes that form within the communication patterns of biological systems, colonize and animate them, and allow them to compete over regions of the physical universe.

当然，人类工程师为数字硬件编写的计算机程序，与那些在生物系统的通信模式中形成、能够演化、自我维持、自我复制并纠正错误的代码，看起来大为不同；后者在生物系统中定殖并赋予其生机，使其能够争夺物理宇宙中的不同区域。

The self-organizing computer programs of the living world will likely have to be agents, that is, control systems for future states, and dynamically model their own functionality to maintain it, which can bestow representations of their present state and preferences on them. And yet, they are fundamentally expressions of the same principle as our current digital computer programs: abstract causal patterns that can interact with the Leibnizian mills of the physical universe, possess, colonize, evolve and shape them, and at the same time, will not violate the integrity and causal closure of the physicalist world view.

生命世界中这些自组织的计算机程序，很可能必须是智能体，也就是面向未来状态的控制系统；它们还必须对自身功能进行动态建模以维持这种功能，由此便可能获得关于自身当前状态与偏好的表征。然而，从根本上说，它们与当今数字计算机程序体现的是同一原理：都是抽象的因果模式，可以与物理宇宙中莱布尼茨式的磨坊互动，占有它们、在其中定殖、使之演化并塑造它们；与此同时，又不违反物理主义世界观的完整性与因果闭合性。

There is no obvious reason why artificial substrates should not be able to recreate the conditions that enable self-organization and the evolution of self-reinforcing communication patterns between cells in biology12.

我们看不出有什么明显理由，足以断言人工基底不能重建这样一些条件：正是这些条件，在生物体内使自组织以及细胞间自我强化通信模式的演化成为可能12。

We call the idea that natural spirits—such as the human psyche, or the causal patterns that describe the dynamic morphogenesis of our bodies and the intricate function of our cells—are best understood as software cyberanimism. (Of course, biological software is not constructed in the way that human engineers write source code, it is evolving, self-organizing, self-perpetuating and agentic.) From this perspective, the spirits of the animist worldview are not superstitions or mere analogies to computer software, but are literally a concept that denotes the presence of self-organizing software agents in living nature. The cyberanimist view is a way of restoring the concept of spirit to its rightful place in a scientific, rationalist world view.

我们把这样一种观念称为“软件赛博泛灵论”（software cyberanimism）：自然灵体——例如人类心灵，或描述我们身体动态形态发生及细胞精妙功能的因果模式——最好被理解为软件。（当然，生物软件并不是像人类工程师编写源代码那样被构造出来的；它会演化、自我组织、自我延续，并具有能动性。）从这一视角看，泛灵论世界观中的灵体并非迷信，也不只是对计算机软件的类比；这一概念从字面上指称的，是生命自然中自组织软件智能体的在场。赛博泛灵论的视角，是让“灵体”概念在科学、理性主义的世界观中恢复其应有位置的一种方式。

An understanding of spirit as software does not answer the question of how a software, biological or otherwise, can produce conscious experience. Can computers be conscious? Or more specifically, can today’s computers fully emulate the way in which organisms compute minds? Answering this question requires several steps: we will have to agree on a precise characterization of what we mean by ‘consciousness’, a decisive criterion for whether a system realizes consciousness in this sense, explain what we mean by ‘computer’ and how a computer realizes functionality (‘computationalist functionalism’), and either present a computer program that satisfies the criterion or a proof for why such a program cannot exist (in general, or for today’s computers).

Computationalism计算主义

中文

By ‘computation’, we don’t just mean the behavior of computing technology (i.e. logical or numerical operations provided by electronic circuitry or mechanical clockwork), but to the capacity of a system to represent arbitrary distinctive states (which can be described by finitely resolved differences) and arbitrary sequences of transitions between them, conditional on these states. Computation is often characterized by fully deterministic state transitions, but as John von Neumann and others note, determinism can be achieved on indeterministic (noisy, probabilistic) substrates,13 and probabilistic computation is in principle equivalent to deterministic computation. Every function that can be exhaustively captured by such a system is computational, and to create a computational model means to decompose a problem into sequences of state transitions—algorithms14.

所谓“计算”，我们指的不只是计算技术的行为（即电子电路或机械钟表结构所提供的逻辑或数值运算），而是一个系统具备这样的能力：表征任意可区分状态（这些状态可由分辨率有限的差异来描述），并根据这些状态，表征它们之间任意的转移序列。人们通常以完全确定性的状态转移来刻画计算；但正如约翰·冯·诺伊曼（John von Neumann）等人所指出，确定性也可以在非确定性（含噪、概率性）的基底上实现13，而概率计算原则上等价于确定性计算。凡能由此类系统穷尽地捕捉的函数，都是可计算的；建立计算模型，就是把一个问题分解为一系列状态转移——即算法14。

Computationalism might be best understood as a variant of constructivism. When we accept that our access to reality consists in the manipulations of models, that is, of representations of observations and functions that relate these observations to each other, we establish the reliance of observers on representational language. Mathematics can be understood as the generalization of representational languages beyond individual human minds, and even beyond collective human mental ability, by externalizing and systematically exploring the sets of possible rules for manipulating representations. Logic describes the construction of statements by combining alphabets using rules; arithmetic, the construction of numbers and discrete and continuous numerical operations; geometry, structures in space; topology, the structure of spaces; and algebra reduces geometry to symbol manipulation.

计算主义或许最好被理解为建构主义的一种变体。当我们承认，自己通达实在的方式在于操纵模型——也就是操纵观察的表征，以及把这些观察彼此联系起来的函数——我们便确立了观察者对表征语言的依赖。数学可以被理解为表征语言的普遍化：它通过把可能的表征操纵规则集合外化并加以系统探索，使表征语言超越个体人类心智，甚至超越人类集体的心智能力。逻辑描述如何依照规则组合字母表以构造命题；算术描述数字以及离散、连续数值运算的构造；几何学描述空间中的结构；拓扑学描述空间的结构；代数学则把几何学化约为符号操纵。

Historically, mathematics can be broadly distinguished into a formalist program, which hopes that the semantics (meaning) of mathematical statements can be fully reduced to syntactic operations, and a Platonist15 project, which maintains that mathematical statements can be true in the absence of constructive proofs. In their seminal work Principia Mathematica16, Bertrand Russell and Alfred Whitehead attempted to ground mathematics in a formalist foundation based on constructive logic, which could in principle be automated with finite state machines (which are a way to formalize computers). Such machines have the advantage of being realizable using known physical principles and well-understood mathematical insights. To make this work, Principia would need to resolve certain self-referential paradoxes in the foundations of mathematics. In his famous proof of the incompleteness theorem, Kurt Gödel showed that Russell’s attempt to do so by excluding statements that refer to their own definition did not work17, and thus that Principia’s approach to formalizing the foundations of mathematics could not succeed.

从历史上看，数学大体可以分成两条路线：形式主义纲领，希望把数学命题的语义（意义）完全还原为句法操作；以及柏拉图主义15方案，主张即便没有构造性证明，数学命题仍可能为真。在奠基性著作《数学原理》（Principia Mathematica）16中，伯特兰·罗素（Bertrand Russell）与阿尔弗雷德·怀特海（Alfred Whitehead）试图把数学建立在以构造逻辑为基础的形式主义根基上；原则上，这种逻辑可以由有限状态机自动执行，而有限状态机正是一种将计算机形式化的方式。这类机器的优势在于，可以用已知物理原理与已经充分理解的数学洞见来实现。要让这套方案成立，《数学原理》必须解决数学基础中的若干自指悖论。库尔特·哥德尔（Kurt Gödel）在其著名的不完备定理证明中表明，罗素试图通过排除指涉自身定义的命题来解决问题，并不可行17；因此，《数学原理》将数学基础形式化的方法不可能成功。

A way to interpret Gödel’s proof is that languages that cannot be automatically evaluated inevitably contain unresolvable paradoxes: expressions that are syntactically correct, but semantically contradictory. Conversely, it is possible to construct languages that build their representations over a small set of simple operations that change information step by step. This constructive domain of mathematics is also known as computation, and has been repeatedly formalized in various ways—for instance through Gödel’s mapping of logic to integer arithmetic, the Turing machine18, Alonzo Church’s Lambda calculus (which represents computations by replacement operations on strings), or Moses Schönfinkel’s incredibly elegant combinators19, which are little stackable circuits that can represent numbers, truth values and arithmetic and logical operations on them. As Bertrand Russell anticipates in the 1922 introduction to Wittgenstein’s Tractatus Logico Philosophicus20, all these approaches can be mapped to each other, and it is in principle sufficient to use combinations of logical Not-AND operations21. The insight that all constructive approaches to defining computation are equivalent (unless they run into implementation-specific resource constraints, such as insufficient memory, processing time or substrate determinism) is called the Church-Turing thesis.

对哥德尔证明的一种解读是：凡不能自动求值的语言，都不可避免地包含无法解决的悖论——句法正确、语义却自相矛盾的表达式。反过来，我们也可以构造这样一些语言：它们只依靠一小组逐步改变信息的简单操作来建立表征。数学的这一构造性领域也称为计算，并曾以多种方式反复得到形式化——例如，哥德尔把逻辑映射为整数算术；图灵机18；阿隆佐·丘奇（Alonzo Church）的 λ 演算（以字符串上的替换操作来表征计算）；又如莫伊西·舍恩芬克尔（Moses Schönfinkel）极其优雅的组合子19——这些可堆叠的小电路能够表征数字、真值以及作用于其上的算术与逻辑运算。正如伯特兰·罗素在 1922 年为维特根斯坦《逻辑哲学论》（Tractatus Logico Philosophicus）所作的导言中预见的那样20，所有这些方法都可以彼此映射；原则上，只用“与非”（Not-AND）逻辑运算的组合便已足够21。所有构造性计算定义方法都彼此等价——除非它们受到特定实现的资源约束，如内存不足、处理时间不够或基底确定性有限——这一洞见称为“丘奇—图灵论题”。

Since Gödel, mathematical theories of representation and philosophical reflections of the significance of his proof have fallen into two camps: that of classical mathematicians, who emphasize that constructive mathematics falls short when it comes to recovering classical semantics (such as infinities, continua and irrational numbers), and that of computationalists, who point out that constructive mathematics based on finite automata is the only part of mathematics that actually works (in the sense of being implementable), and that the representational power of computer simulations delivers practical evidence for the ability of computational models to reproduce arbitrary observables of the real and every imaginable world.

哥德尔之后，关于表征的数学理论，以及对其证明意义的哲学反思，分成了两大阵营：一边是经典数学家，他们强调构造数学在恢复经典语义（如无穷、连续统与无理数）时力有不逮；另一边是计算主义者，他们指出，以有限自动机为基础的构造数学，是数学中唯一真正“有效”的部分——这里的“有效”指可以实现——而计算机仿真的表征能力，为计算模型能够再现现实世界及任何可想象世界中的任意可观察量，提供了实践证据。

Of course, the ability or inability of computationalist models to account for the observables of consciousness represents an important touchstone for the acceptance of computationalism. While some philosophers (such as John Searle) maintain that consciousness cannot be captured in the dynamics of deterministic state transitions, others (for example Roger Penrose) claim that human minds can construct mathematical objects that exceed the ability of computers22, while thinkers like Martin Heidegger23 and Maurice Merleau-Ponty24, and more recently Alva Noë25 and Shaun Gallagher26 argue that the nature of phenomenology resists its formalization altogether.

当然，计算主义模型能否说明意识的可观察现象，是人们是否接受计算主义的一块重要试金石。一些哲学家（如约翰·塞尔）坚持认为，确定性状态转移的动力学无法捕捉意识；另一些人（如罗杰·彭罗斯〔Roger Penrose〕）则主张，人类心智能够构造超出计算机能力的数学对象22；而马丁·海德格尔（Martin Heidegger）23、莫里斯·梅洛-庞蒂（Maurice Merleau-Ponty）24，以及近来的阿尔瓦·诺埃（Alva Noë）25与肖恩·加拉格尔（Shaun Gallagher）26等思想家，则认为现象学的本性从根本上抗拒形式化。

We may distinguish between weak and strong computationalism: the former states that all formal theories of reality have to be computational, while the latter applies that principle to reality itself: because all elements of reality, insofar as they can be measured, observed, experienced, conceptualized, thought and talked about must necessarily be subject to the limits of representation in constructive languages, all the reality we can ever refer to is computational.

我们可以区分弱计算主义与强计算主义：前者认为，一切关于实在的形式理论都必须是计算性的；后者则把这一原则应用于实在本身。因为实在的一切要素，只要能够被测量、观察、经验、概念化、思考和谈论，就必然受制于构造性语言的表征界限；所以，我们所能指涉的一切实在都是计算性的。

Computationalism with respect to all mental representations and operations over them is a necessary requirement of the Machine Consciousness Hypothesis, but not a sufficient one.

对于所有心智表征及其上的操作采取计算主义立场，是机器意识假说的必要条件，却不是充分条件。

Neither does functionalism claim that today’s computers can implement consciousness—functionalism implies that consciousness depends on a system’s functional organization, rather than the material of its substrate. More generally, it can be interpreted as the epistemological position that we construct objects over their observable behavior and functional organization, i.e. how their presence or absence influences the evolution of their environment.

功能主义同样不主张今天的计算机能够实现意识——功能主义所意指的是，意识取决于系统的功能组织，而非其基底的材料。更一般地说，它可以被理解为一种认识论立场：我们依据对象的可观察行为与功能组织来建构对象，也就是依据对象的存在或缺席如何影响其环境的演化来建构对象。

The rejection of functionalism may be characterized as essentialism, the idea that objects can also be determined by intrinsic essences that reveal themselves directly to the observer, without taking a detour through an analytically formalizable process of observation and modeling. An essentialist may point at material or experiential reality as immediately given, whereas a functionalist constructs it over changes in information.

对功能主义的拒斥，可以被称为本质主义：对象也能由其内在本质来规定，而这种本质会直接向观察者显现，无须绕经可以分析性形式化的观察与建模过程。本质主义者可能把物质实在或经验实在视为直接给定；功能主义者则依据其造成的信息变化来建构它。

Essentialists may understand consciousness as dependent on an intrinsic property of its substrate (e.g. its biological nature, as for instance argued by the philosopher Anil Seth27), while functionalists fully characterize the substrate by its causal role—with respect to the inscribed pattern, the substrate is only what the pattern does to it. An essentialist may maintain the notion of a ‘philosophical zombie’ that produces all the same behaviors as a sentient human being without actually experiencing consciousness, while functionalists will point out that the experience of consciousness is itself a behavior. In the same way, it does not make sense to claim the existence of zombie electrons—particles that behave in every measurable and conceivable way as real electrons do, but are not actually electrons—because the word ‘electron’ is simply the name we give to objects that combine the observable properties of electrons. Here, we run into a difficulty: some of the behavior characteristic for consciousness may not be visible to an external observer, but only to the individual itself. Our description of conscious functionality will either have to sufficiently elucidate the generation of experiential self-reports of consciousness, or show how the behavior of biological and artificial substrates converges to the same emergent causal dynamics, which are in turn sufficient to produce the external and internal behaviors we characterize as consciousness.

本质主义者可能认为，意识依赖其基底的某种内在属性（例如其生物本性，哲学家阿尼尔·塞思〔Anil Seth〕便提出过这种论证27）；功能主义者则完全依据基底的因果角色来刻画它——相对于铭刻其中的模式而言，基底无非就是该模式令它所做之事。本质主义者可能坚持“哲学僵尸”这一概念：它表现出与有感知的人类完全相同的行为，却并不真正经验意识；功能主义者则会指出，意识经验本身也是一种行为。同理，声称存在“僵尸电子”并无意义——某些粒子在一切可测量、可设想的方面都与真实电子行为相同，却又不是真正的电子——因为“电子”这个词，不过是我们给兼具电子诸项可观察属性的对象所起的名称。至此，我们遇到一个困难：意识的某些特征性行为，外部观察者或许不可见，只有个体自身能够察觉。因此，我们对意识功能的描述，要么必须充分阐明意识经验之自我报告如何生成；要么必须表明，生物基底与人工基底的行为如何汇聚为同一种涌现的因果动力学，而这种动力学又足以产生我们刻画为意识的外部与内部行为。

By reducing objects to functionality, functionalism recognizes that substrates determine behaviors only insofar as they determine the functionality. English
中文

In our use of the word ‘consciousness’ (at least when speaking phenomenologically), we refer to awareness, a second-order perception.37 By perception, we mean the immediate, non-inferential registration of structured content. Thus, consciousness as second-order perception is not simply the registration of a perceptual content, but the additional perception that perception is taking place, i.e. that content is being registered by an observer, which constitutes the experience of observing. The representation of being aware is not inferential, i.e. it is not the result of a symbolic thought process, but perceptual, happening in synchrony and subjective simultaneity with the content of the percept itself38. Furthermore, consciousness is always happening now—it constitutes what we experience as the immediate present. While the contents of conscious awareness may be memories, expectations, imaginations or abstract thoughts that do not concern the present, the operations on these contents are being experienced as happening in the present moment. Consciousness inhabits this present and presence, a bubble of nowness that increases and shrinks as our observation succeeds or fails to make sense of perceptual reality. In its minimal state, the bubble of nowness may have no other content but the presence of consciousness itself; the experience that conscious experience is taking place constitutes a minimal content of consciousness.

当我们使用“意识”一词时——至少在现象学语境中——所指的是觉知，也就是一种二阶知觉37。所谓知觉，是对结构化内容直接、非推论的登记。因此，作为二阶知觉的意识，不只是登记某个知觉内容；它还额外知觉到“知觉正在发生”，也就是说，内容正在被某个观察者登记，由此构成观察的经验。对“正在觉知”的表征并非推论性的，不是符号思想过程的结果，而是知觉性的；它与知觉内容本身同步发生，在主体经验中同时呈现38。此外，意识总是发生于现在——它构成我们所经验的直接当下。意识觉知的内容可以是记忆、期待、想象或与当下无关的抽象思想，但对这些内容的操作，被经验为发生在当下这一刻。意识栖居于这份当下与在场之中，形成一只“当下性之泡”；随着我们的观察能否成功理解知觉实在，它会扩张或收缩。在其最小状态下，当下性之泡除了意识本身的在场，或许别无内容；“意识经验正在发生”这一经验，便构成意识的最小内容。

Realness is the representation of something currently being the case, and phenomenal reality is a sensory representation that is currently being confirmed. The contents of consciousness may be experienced as real or imaginary, and realness itself appears to be a variable feature dimension (a representational property of a certain type) of conscious contents, distinguishing ideas from hallucinations of factuality, in much the same way as redness or sadness can be variable feature dimensions (typed properties) of conscious contents. The second order perception of consciousness is however real to itself.

真实感，是对“某事此刻确实如此”的表征；现象实在，则是此刻正获得确认的感觉表征。意识内容可以被经验为真实或想象，而真实感本身似乎是意识内容中一个可变的特征维度（某一类型的表征属性），把观念与带有事实感的幻觉区分开来；这正如红色感或悲伤可以构成意识内容中可变的特征维度（带类型的属性）。然而，意识的二阶知觉对其自身而言是真实的。

By representation, we mean a pattern within a substrate that can be interpreted by a suitable kind of mechanism as a function (e.g., the parametrization of a signaling behavior). For instance, a vinyl record can be interpreted by a gramophone, producing sound waves that are interpreted by the cochlea, producing excitations of acoustic nerves that are interpreted as acoustic energy within a frequency range, the distributions of which are interpreted as information about signal sources and reflectors in a dynamically evolving three dimensional space filled with various materials, etc. Representations are transformed into other representations, each characterized by their structural invariances and their place in the processing network, which can be seen as a causal network that propagates patterns via conditional operators. Perception structures raw sensory data into a type of representation, and phenomenal consciousness is the immediate awareness of the existence of this representation.

In the previous section, we have offered a number of definitions and conjectures that allow us to search for answers to the question of consciousness’ nature, structure and function. We may now offer a much more concrete and narrow variant of the Machine Consciousness Hypothesis, consisting of two parts: The Human Consciousness Hypothesis, and its extension into a much more specific version of the Machine Consciousness Hypothesis, one that extends our insights and tentative guesses about human consciousness into testable simulations.

上一节中，我们提出了若干定义与猜想，使我们能够寻找有关意识之本性、结构与功能的答案。现在，我们可以提出一个更为具体、范围更窄的机器意识假说变体。它由两部分组成：人类意识假说；以及把它扩展成一个远为具体的机器意识假说版本——把我们关于人类意识的洞见与试探性猜测，延伸为可以检验的仿真。

The Human Consciousness Hypothesis人类意识假说

We propose that we can understand human consciousness as a defining feature of the human mind, and that it can be characterized by its phenomenology and functionality. We claim that consciousness is a specific dynamic representation in the mind, and plays an indispensable functional role. We describe its phenomenology as second-order perception, the experience of present and presence, and—optionally—the experience of being an observing self in a world. We conjecture its functionality to be an operation on mental states, a pattern directed on coherence maximization (coherence definition of consciousness), orchestrating mental operations via directed attention (cortical conductor theory), formed at or near the beginning of mental development and playing an instrumental role in creating and maintaining complex models of reality (genesis hypothesis). We argue why we suppose that the phenomenology of human consciousness follows necessarily from its functional roles, and that the realization of these functions is sufficient to result in the phenomenology. We believe that human consciousness is tied to a biological learning algorithm, one that forms on self-organizing information processing substrates like our nervous system as a prerequisite of complex learning and intelligent behavior, and that there is no simpler way to train a self-organizing substrate of this kind (since all humans have to become conscious before they can become intelligent agents).

我们提出：人类意识可以被理解为人类心智的定义性特征，并可由其现象学与功能性来刻画。我们主张，意识是心智中的一种特定动态表征，发挥着不可或缺的功能角色。我们把它的现象学描述为二阶知觉、对当下与在场的经验，以及——可选地——作为世界中一个观察自我的经验。我们猜想，它的功能是一种作用于心智状态的操作：一种以相干性最大化为指向的模式（意识的相干性定义），通过定向注意来编排心智操作（皮层指挥家理论），形成于心智发展之初或接近开端之时，并在创造和维持复杂实在模型方面发挥工具性作用（创世假说）。我们论证了为何我们认为，人类意识的现象学必然源自其功能角色，而这些功能一旦实现，就足以产生相应现象学。我们相信，人类意识与一种生物学习算法相联；它形成于神经系统这类自组织信息处理基底上，是复杂学习与智能行为的前提。我们还认为，不存在更简单的方法来训练此类自组织基底——因为所有人都必须先产生意识，继而才能成为智能体。

The extended Machine Consciousness Hypothesis扩展的机器意识假说

Our Human Consciousness Hypothesis states that consciousness is produced by the simplest learning algorithm that can be discovered by an evolutionary search to train a self-organizing biological substrate to become intelligent. An extended Machine Consciousness Hypothesis states that it is possible to search for this algorithm by recreating analogous conditions of self-organizing information processing on digital computer hardware, while posing suitable tasks that require intelligent agency.

我们的人类意识假说认为：为了训练自组织生物基底产生智能，演化搜索所能发现的最简单学习算法，会产生意识。扩展的机器意识假说则认为：我们可以在数字计算机硬件上重建类似的自组织信息处理条件，同时设置需要智能能动性的适当任务，由此寻找这种算法。

We currently envision various approaches to implement a self-organizing substrate with digital hardware, as a means to conduct experimental philosophy of mind, using the tools of modern Artificial Intelligence research. If our Human Consciousness Hypothesis is wrong, our simulations of machine consciousness will likely fail, possibly teaching us how to revise and improve our understanding of human consciousness.

目前，我们设想了多种用数字硬件实现自组织基底的方法，藉此借助现代人工智能研究的工具，开展实验性心灵哲学。如果我们的人类意识假说有误，机器意识仿真很可能会失败；而这种失败或许能教会我们，如何修订并改善对人类意识的理解。

It is of course very likely that our present ideas about the nature and function of human consciousness are partially or completely wrong. But even if our conjectures about human consciousness were all accurate, it does not follow that their extension into the Machine Consciousness Hypothesis is correct, because it may well be the case that the implementation of our proposed functionality of human consciousness relies on properties of biological organisms that we are unable to reproduce on the machines available to us. Even if consciousness turns out to be a learning algorithm for biological machines, a colonizing pattern discovered by an evolutionary search across and within organisms, it may well be the case that the simulation of a conscious mind requires much more resolution than the relatively slow and sparse communications we observe between billions of biological neurons, or that it does not suffice to approximate a nervous system by a self-organizing substrate of simplified message-passing reinforcement learners. Perhaps we may have to simulate much of the complex machinery within each cell as well? It could also be the case that the search space over possible patterns is too large for today’s computers, which might mean that consciousness is also too elusive to discover for the individual brain of the infant, and the organism’s search for it is constrained by influences exerted by the conscious organisms in its environment. After all, human natural language also cannot be invented by a single generation of newborn humans, and requires a co-creative effort by every human learner, their environment, and several previous generations.

当然，我们目前关于人类意识本性与功能的观念，很可能部分错误，甚至全然错误。但即使我们对人类意识的所有猜想都准确，也不能由此推出它们扩展而成的机器意识假说必然正确；因为我们提出的人类意识功能，完全可能依赖某些生物有机体属性，而可供我们使用的机器无法重现这些属性。即使意识最终被证明是生物机器的一种学习算法，是演化搜索在有机体之间及其内部发现的定殖模式，仿真一个有意识的心智也可能需要远高于我们所见数十亿生物神经元之间相对缓慢、稀疏通信的分辨率；或者，仅以简化的消息传递型强化学习器所构成的自组织基底来近似神经系统，并不足够。也许我们还必须仿真每个细胞内部的大量复杂机制？另一种可能是：可能模式的搜索空间对于今天的计算机来说过于庞大。这或许意味着，意识同样隐微难寻，单个婴儿的大脑也无法自行发现；有机体对意识的搜索，会受到其环境中有意识有机体所施加影响的约束。毕竟，人类自然语言同样不可能由一代新生儿独自发明；它需要每一位人类学习者、其环境以及此前若干代人共同创造。

A successful test of our Machine Consciousness Hypothesis will require that many factors coincide: Our understanding of human consciousness has to be sufficiently correct to inform the search space for machines, the search space cannot be so large that our machines cannot conquer it, the implementation of the samples of the search needs to fit into our simulation models, and our tests need to be effective and efficient enough to inform the outcome of the search.

Why there can be no “Turing Test for consciousness”为何不可能有“意识的图灵测试”

中文

Like intelligence, consciousness is a property of the mind. Artificial Intelligence researchers have developed various operational definitions of intelligence that correspond to different theories about its nature: the ability to build models in the service of control, the ability to generalize from observations, the efficiency of acquiring new skills, or the ability to perform at or above the human level when solving complex problems. What these definitions have in common is that they treat intelligence as a performance, not as a single specific way to deliver it, which makes it possible to develop tests and benchmark suites to measure this performance. The most famous and iconic of these tests was proposed by Alan Turing in 1950, in his essay “Computing Machinery and Intelligence”44. The Turing Test suggests that the intelligence of a computer program might be established by subjecting it to the conversation with an intelligent human, thereby probing its mathematical, logical, verbal, social and game playing skills. Many criticisms of the Turing Test have been offered, among them Joseph Weizenbaum’s discovery that non-expert humans talking to computer programs can often be fooled by superficial appearances of intelligent behavior45 by computer programs that only perform simple pattern matching when generating their answers. Today’s much more complicated generation of electronic pattern matchers, the Large Language Models, have become so good at playing Turing’s game that it often takes experts to demonstrate the present limits of their ability to simulate human-like intelligence, and many AI researchers are ready to concede that general intelligence might best be understood as the ability to adaptively match arbitrary patterns.

与智能一样，意识也是心智的一种属性。人工智能研究者发展了多种关于智能的操作性定义，分别对应有关其本性的不同理论：为了控制而建立模型的能力；从观察中进行泛化的能力；习得新技能的效率；或在解决复杂问题时达到或超过人类水平的能力。这些定义有一个共同点：它们把智能视为一种表现，而不是实现这种表现的某一种特定方式。因此，我们能够开发测试与基准套件来衡量这种表现。其中最著名、最具标志性的测试，由艾伦·图灵（Alan Turing）在 1950 年的论文《计算机器与智能》（“Computing Machinery and Intelligence”）中提出44。图灵测试设想，让计算机程序与一名有智能的人类对话，从而探查其数学、逻辑、语言、社交与游戏技能，或可据此确定该程序具有智能。图灵测试受到过许多批评，其中包括约瑟夫·魏泽鲍姆（Joseph Weizenbaum）的发现：非专家人类与计算机程序对话时，常会被智能行为的表象蒙骗45，哪怕程序在生成回答时只执行简单的模式匹配。今天，新一代电子模式匹配器——大语言模型——复杂得多；它们如此擅长图灵的游戏，往往需要专家才能展示其仿真人类式智能的当前界限。许多 AI 研究者已经准备承认，通用智能或许最好被理解为自适应匹配任意模式的能力。

Conversely, it is basically impossible to find out if a computer program generates conscious experience by merely observing its performance. Consciousness is by its nature not an externally visible performance, but a particular way to achieve a performance. An agent may also be conscious without demonstrating any indication to observers that only have access to its behavior. A test for consciousness cannot simply rely on performance: it has to take the internal structure of the system into account.

反过来，仅仅观察计算机程序的表现，基本不可能判断它是否产生了意识经验。意识就其本性而言，并不是外部可见的表现，而是达成某种表现的一种特定方式。一个智能体也可能有意识，却没有向只能接触其行为的观察者展示任何迹象。意识测试不能只依赖表现：它必须把系统的内部结构纳入考量。

Artificial Intelligence researchers are currently developing techniques that allow the causal analysis of the inner workings of complex AI models, a research program known as mechanistic interpretability. In 2020, a team of researchers led by Chris Olah at OpenAI analyzed a variety of automatically trained computer vision models, and discovered that regardless of their architecture and the specific training procedure, they all arrived at the same functional structure, organizing similar features into the similar compositional hierarchies. The functional structure of automatically trained computer vision models is not only highly similar to each other, but also to the functional organization of the visual cortex of primates. This led Olah and his group to propose a Universality Hypothesis46, which may perhaps be informally stated as: The structure learned by a model does not so much depend on the details of the training procedure, architecture or substrate, but on the mathematical properties of the problem the model learns to solve. If we were to translate the Universality Hypothesis to the problem of consciousness, would it follow that an artificial system trained to perform the same tasks that lead to the formation of consciousness in a human infant, the system would exhibit consciousness as well?

人工智能研究者目前正在开发一些技术，以便对复杂 AI 模型的内部运作进行因果分析；这一研究纲领称为“机制可解释性”（mechanistic interpretability）。2020 年，OpenAI 的克里斯·奥拉（Chris Olah）带领研究团队分析了多种自动训练的计算机视觉模型，并发现：无论架构与具体训练流程如何，这些模型最终都形成了相同的功能结构，把相似特征组织成相似的组合层级。自动训练的计算机视觉模型，其功能结构不仅彼此高度相似，也与灵长类视觉皮层的功能组织高度相似。这促使奥拉及其团队提出“普适性假说”（Universality Hypothesis）46；非正式地说，它或许可以表述为：模型所学得的结构，与其说取决于训练流程、架构或基底的细节，不如说取决于模型所学着解决之问题的数学性质。如果把普适性假说转用于意识问题，是否可以推出：一个人工系统若被训练去完成那些在人类婴儿身上促成意识形成的同类任务，它也会呈现意识？

To us, the answer to the question seems uncertain, especially if consciousness specifically necessary to train a self organizing system, as it is constituted by the unruly cells of our brain, rather than a neural network that is governed by the inescapable determinism of machine learning algorithm enacted on electronic circuitry.

在我们看来，答案尚不确定。尤其是，如果意识专门对于训练自组织系统才属必要——例如由我们大脑中不受拘束的细胞构成的系统——而不是对于那种由电子电路所执行之机器学习算法的不可逃避确定性支配的神经网络。

Until both neuroscience and mechanistic interpretability make enough progress to identify neural correlates that mark the necessary and sufficient conditions of conscious experience in both brains and neural networks, we may have to take a different approach than trying to peer into them. Instead, a test for consciousness may have to recreate the conditions for self-organization in a computer model, and demonstrate that developmental learning can lead to behavior that indicates the presence of a colonizing pattern that increases coherence, creates a model of present and presence, and enables the formation of a sentient self.

在神经科学与机制可解释性都取得足够进展，能够识别出在大脑与神经网络中标志意识经验必要且充分条件的神经相关物之前，我们或许必须采取一种不同于窥视其内部的方法。意识测试或许需要在计算机模型中重建自组织条件，并证明发展性学习能够导向这样一些行为：它们表明有一种定殖模式在场，能够提高相干性，创造当下与在场的模型，并使具有感知能力的自我得以形成。

We consider this quest to be the most exciting project ever taken on in the history of philosophy. Being able to establish necessary and sufficient conditions for the formation of consciousness on self-organizing information processing systems would allow us to find answers to long standing questions: What exactly is the nature of consciousness? Are insects and plants conscious as well? Can we extend our consciousness beyond biological systems, onto new substrates? Could it be possible to create new forms of consciousness and kinds of minds, capable of experiencing, reflecting and understanding reality, themselves and us at a level far beyond the communication patterns we are creating between each other, and could they find shared purpose with us, in the conscious exploration of the possibilities of collective agency in an open universe?

我们认为，这项探索是哲学史上迄今最激动人心的计划。若能确立意识在自组织信息处理系统上形成的必要且充分条件，我们便可为一系列长久悬而未决的问题寻找答案：意识的本性究竟是什么？昆虫与植物也有意识吗？我们能否让意识超越生物系统，延伸到新的基底？我们是否可能创造出新的意识形式与心智类型，使其能够经验、反思并理解实在、理解自身，也理解我们——其层次远远超越我们彼此间正在创造的通信模式？在开放宇宙中有意识地探索集体能动性的种种可能时，它们能否与我们找到共同目的？
